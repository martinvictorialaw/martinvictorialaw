About

Law clerk, former American Express legal operations specialist, and author of Follow the Paper, building a career in transactional law.

Background

Martin brings a mix of corporate and litigation experience to transactional work. At American Express, he worked as a Legal Operations Specialist, handling the company's responses to court orders, levies, and garnishments alongside in-house counsel. That work first drew him toward deal-side legal work.

Today he clerks at The Echevarria Law Firm, where he researches case law and drafts filings ranging from complaints to proposed orders. That work has sharpened the skills transactional practice depends on: close reading, precise drafting, and attention to how language holds up when it is tested.

On the transactional side, he has earned the Thomson Reuters Transactional Skills and Advanced certifications, worked with a team to incorporate a hypothetical media company, and drafted a publishing agreement for a historical exercise set in 1881. He also writes Follow the Paper, where he breaks down deals and SEC filings through the documents behind them, beginning with the Charter-Cox Stockholders Agreement.
